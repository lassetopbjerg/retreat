Everything you need to know

The days
10–13
Painting from the live model: light, shadow and colour mixing
13–16
Break: lunch, rest, a swim
16–
Optional painting outdoors in the hills, by the coast or in the garden

On rainy days the atelier stays open.

Your teacher

Jan-Ove Tuv is a Norwegian painter and longtime student of Odd Nerdrum. He paints and teaches in the classical, old-master tradition from his studio on the south coast of Norway, and hosts the podcast The Cave of Apelles.

He teaches in English and Norwegian.

Where to stay

Lodging is not included, which keeps the price down. Choose what suits you:

  • Bring a tent and camp in the Rødegaard garden for a small nightly fee
  • A room at Rødegaard / Jordens Sang: kontakt@jordenssang.dk
  • Molsgaarden B&B, Kildevej 26, Knebel
Food

There is a small kitchen in the atelier where you can cook. There are no shops in Knebel. Groceries are in Vrinners or Ebeltoft, about 20 minutes by bus.

Getting there

Rødegaard, Karlshøjvej 5, 8420 Knebel, Denmark. About 45 minutes by car from Aarhus.

From Norway, the Larvik–Hirtshals ferry is the easiest route.

What to bring
  • Your own oil painting materials. You get the full list when you reserve.
  • A canvas for the model painting
  • An easel or pochade box for painting outdoors
