Dagene

Formiddagene går til fordybelse ved staffeliet, med Jan-Ove ved din side. Resten af dagen er din egen.

Vi taler om maleri og tankerne bag, tager praktiske spørgsmål og giver feedback på det, du har malet i din fritid.

Åbent Atelier

Du er velkommen til at male selv, eller se Jan-Ove male. Hvis vejret tillader det, er der rig mulighed for at finde flotte landskabsmotiver i området. Ellers kan vi male indenfor i atelieret. Der er ellers ingen undervisning i dette tidsrum.

Atelieret er åbent hele dagen og om aftenen, og du kan tage dine egne projekter med. Der er plads til spontane udflugter og hyggelige stunder omkring bålet.

Kommer du langvejs fra, så regn med at ankomme dagen før. Søndag slutter vi kl. 13.

Din underviser

Jan-Ove Tuv

Jan-Ove (f. 1976) er norsk maler og var assistent for Odd Nerdrum fra 1996 til 2002. Han har en dyb forståelse for maleriets grundprincipper, kunsthistorie, myter og fortællinger, har skrevet bøger om kitsch og medvirket i dokumentarserien «Jakten på Nerdrum».

Jan-Ove har en evne til at gøre komplekse idéer og teknikker let fordøjelige, hvilket gør ham til en fantastisk underviser. Uanset hvor du er på din rejse som maler, tager du hjem med ny inspiration, viden og et fornyet perspektiv på kunstens formål, kunnen og mening.

I videoen herunder forklarer han kort Apelles-paletten, som vi også arbejder med på kurset.

Apelles-palettenVideo med Jan-Ove Tuv · YouTube
Mere om Jan-Ove

Han har afholdt kurser i 16 år og tilpasser undervisningen til alle, fra komplette nybegyndere til øvede kunstnere. Han underviser på norsk og engelsk.

Hans motto er «Enkle regler – enkelt forklart». Du lærer at male naturtro med en reduceret palet med få farver, og han tilpasser målene til den enkelte elev, uanset niveau.

Stedet

Rødegaard

Rødegaard ligger i Vistoft ved Nationalpark Mols Bjerge, med bakker, skov og hav tæt på, og den gamle købstad Ebeltoft ligger ca. 20 minutter væk i bil eller bus.

Fotos: Jordens Sang

Oplev Mols og Helgenæs

Eftermiddagene er dine. Tag staffeliet med ud i bakkerne eller ned til vandet, eller gå på opdagelse. Tryk på et sted for at se billeder og finde vej.

Nationalpark Mols Bjerge

  • Trehøje (127 m): tre gravhøje fra bronzealderen med udsigt helt fra Aarhus til Ebeltoft
  • Agri Bavnehøj (137 m): det højeste punkt i Mols Bjerge
  • Poskær Stenhus ved Knebel: Danmarks største runddysse fra stenalderen
  • Tinghulen: et 30–35 meter dybt dødishul, hvor tre sogne i gamle dage holdt ting
  • Den Italienske Sti ved Molslaboratoriet: høje enebærbuske og et landskab, der næsten ligner Middelhavet
  • Fuglsø Strand og Femmøller Strand: skrænter, lange gåture og en dukkert

Helgenæs

  • Sletterhage Fyr: det gamle fyr yderst på Helgenæs, med udsigt ud over Aarhus Bugt
  • Dragsmur: her er Helgenæs kun 240 meter bred, og ifølge sagnet trak vikingerne deres skibe over land. Et godt sted at sætte kajakken i vandet
  • Ellemandsbjerget: flot udsigt over Aarhus Bugt
  • Strandene: her kan du finde sten, som isen har slæbt med fra Norge og Sverige

Ebeltoft og Kalø

  • Ebeltoft: gammel købstad med brostensgader, Glasmuseet og Fregatten Jylland
  • Kalø Slotsruin: ruinen af en middelalderborg på en lille halvø i Kalø Vig. Man går derud ad en gammel dæmning

The days

Time to learn – time to immerse yourself

Mornings are for deep, focused work at the easel, with Jan-Ove at your side. The rest of the day is yours.

10–10.45

Morning meeting

We talk about painting and the thinking behind it, take practical questions and give feedback on what you have painted in your free time.

10.45–13

Nude model

Life painting from the nude model: light, shadow and colour mixing.

13–15

Break

Lunch, rest, a swim. The beach and the national park are right outside the door.

15–

Open Studio

You are welcome to paint on your own, or watch Jan-Ove paint. If the weather allows, there are plenty of fine landscape subjects in the area. Otherwise we can paint indoors in the studio. There is no teaching during these hours.

The studio is open all day and into the evening, and you are welcome to bring your own projects. There is room for spontaneous outings and cosy evenings around the campfire.

If you are travelling from far away, plan to arrive the day before. On Sunday we finish at 13:00.

Your teacher

Jan-Ove Tuv

Jan-Ove (b. 1976) is a Norwegian painter who worked as Odd Nerdrum’s assistant from 1996 to 2002. He has a deep understanding of the fundamentals of painting, art history, myth and storytelling. He has written books on kitsch and appeared in the documentary series “Jakten på Nerdrum” (The Hunt for Nerdrum).

Jan-Ove has a gift for making complex ideas and techniques easy to grasp, which makes him a wonderful teacher. Wherever you are as a painter, you will leave with new inspiration and a fresh perspective on painting.

In the video below, he gives a short introduction to the Apelles palette, which we will use during the course.

The Apelles paletteVideo with Jan-Ove Tuv · YouTube
More about Jan-Ove

He has been teaching courses for 16 years and tailors his teaching to each student, from complete beginners to advanced artists. He teaches in Norwegian and English.

His motto is “Simple rules, simply explained.” You learn to paint naturalistically with a limited palette of just a few colours, and he sets individual goals for each student, whatever their level.

The place

Rødegaard

Rødegaard is in Vistoft, by Mols Bjerge National Park, with hills, woods and the sea close by, and the old market town of Ebeltoft about 20 minutes away by car or bus.

Photos: Jordens Sang

Explore Mols and Helgenæs

The afternoons are yours. Take your easel out into the hills or down to the water, or go exploring. Tap a place to see pictures and find your way.

Mols Bjerge National Park

  • Trehøje, 127 m: three Bronze Age burial mounds and a view from Aarhus to Ebeltoft
  • Agri Bavnehøj, 137 m: the highest point in the national park
  • Poskær Stenhus near Knebel: the largest round dolmen in Denmark, from the Stone Age
  • Tinghulen: a kettle hole 30–35 metres deep, where three parishes once held court
  • The Italian Path from the Mols Laboratory: tall junipers and views that recall the Mediterranean
  • Fuglsø and Femmøller beaches: cliffs, walks and swimming

Helgenæs

  • Sletterhage Lighthouse: the old lighthouse at the tip of the peninsula, looking out over the Bay of Aarhus
  • Dragsmur: the narrowest point of the peninsula, only 240 metres wide, where the Vikings are said to have dragged their ships overland. A good place to start a kayak trip
  • Ellemandsbjerget: a view across the Bay of Aarhus
  • The beaches: stones carried here by the ice from Norway and Sweden

Ebeltoft and Kalø

  • Ebeltoft: an old market town with cobbled streets, the Glass Museum and the frigate Jylland
  • Kalø Castle Ruin: the ruin of a medieval castle on a small peninsula in Kalø Vig, reached on foot along an old causeway

What to bring
  • Oil paint in four colours: black, white, yellow and red
  • Painting medium: linseed oil and turpentine, or an odourless thinner
  • A palette and a small cup for the medium
  • Brushes: a handful of flat brushes in a few sizes, some stiff and some soft
  • Canvas: one or two, up to 50 × 70 cm
  • An easel, preferably one you can carry outdoors. If you don't have one, you can borrow one from Lasse. Just let him know in advance
  • Small things: rags, charcoal and a small hand mirror

You can start with what you already have, and you don't need to buy anything expensive.
