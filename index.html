Din underviser

Jan-Ove Tuv (f. 1976) er norsk maler. Fra 1996 til 2002 var han assistent for Odd Nerdrum, og han har undervist i maleri i omkring 14 år.

Han maler portrætter og figurer i olie, akvarel, tegning og grafik. Han har skrevet bøger om kitsch, været med i dokumentarserien «Jakten på Nerdrum» og står bag podcasten The Cave of Apelles.

Han underviser på norsk og engelsk.

Sådan underviser han

Hans motto er «Enkle regler – enkelt forklart». Du lærer at male naturtro med en reduceret palet med få farver, og han tilpasser målene til den enkelte elev, uanset niveau.

Det eneste, du skal have med af forkundskaber, er lysten til at lære.

«Han har en evne til at formidle budskabet med grundighed og ro.» — Edith Maier Kihle, tidligere elev

Your teacher

Jan-Ove Tuv (b. 1976) is a Norwegian painter. From 1996 to 2002 he was assistant to Odd Nerdrum, and he has taught painting for about 14 years.

He paints portraits and figures in oil, watercolour, drawing and printmaking. He has written books on kitsch, appeared in the documentary series «Jakten på Nerdrum» (The Hunt for Nerdrum) and hosts the podcast The Cave of Apelles.

He teaches in English and Norwegian.

How he teaches

His motto is "Simple rules, simply explained." You learn to paint naturalistically with a reduced palette of few colours, and he sets goals for each student, whatever their level.

The only thing you need to bring in terms of experience is the wish to learn.

"He has a gift for getting the message across with thoroughness and calm." — Edith Maier Kihle, former student

Prefer to cater for yourself? There is a small kitchen in the atelier. There are no shops in Knebel. Groceries are in Vrinners or Ebeltoft, about 20 minutes by bus.
